Embeddables includes features that help you run a HIPAA-compliant workspace. Use this page with the in-product checklist.

<Tip>
  The **Compliance** tab in the Embeddables Web App lists every step needed for full HIPAA compliance. Complete that checklist for your workspace.
</Tip>

## Set team permissions

Roles and data access are the foundation of compliance. See [User Roles & Permissions](/guides/user-roles-permissions) for the full model.

- Assign one trusted **Data Privacy Officer** to manage privacy settings and the HIPAA checklist.
- Give **Full Data Access** only to people with a clear need for PII/PHI. Everyone else should use **Anonymized Data Access**.
- Review team permissions regularly as people and requirements change.

## Label sensitive fields

For each live Embeddable, label which fields are:

- **Health data** (PHI)
- **Personally identifiable data** (PII)
- **Contact info** (for example, email or phone)

The Data Privacy Officer defines these labels per Embeddable. Labeling controls who can see sensitive data. Incorrectly labeled fields stay hidden from everyone except users with Full Data Access.

<Note>
  When defining field types, the Data Privacy Officer should follow HHS guidance on de-identification:

  [Guidance Regarding Methods for De-identification of Protected Health Information](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/special-topics/de-identification/index.html)
</Note>

<Warning>
  Keep your list of live (published) Embeddables up to date. Use that list as the checklist of Embeddables that need field labeling.
</Warning>

## Ongoing responsibility

HIPAA compliance is continuous. The Data Privacy Officer should ensure that:

- New Embeddables are reviewed and labeled before going live.
- Existing Embeddables stay current when fields or data requirements change.
- Team permissions are reviewed and updated as needed.
