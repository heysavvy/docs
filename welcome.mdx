## One system to build and improve your entire customer journey

Embeddables provides a self-improving front-end infrastructure specifically designed for your health and wellness brand to build and optimize high-conversion intake funnels. While building a basic funnel may be straightforward, we solve the complex technical infrastructure required for you to scale, maintain compliance, and systematically improve conversion rates.

Key aspects of the platform include:

- **Versatile Building Tools**: Your team can use a no-code visual builder for drag-and-drop configuration, or your developers can use the CLI to build custom components in React and CSS. The entire system integrates into your website with just two lines of code.
- **Performance and Optimization**: The platform is built to increase your patient completion rates, with clients seeing an average 3.2x increase in conversions. It features automated testing suggestions and split flows that allow your funnel to improve over time with minimal engineering effort.
- **Health-Grade Security**: We offer a HIPAA-compliant infrastructure for the secure collection and storage of your customers' Protected Health Information (PHI). It also includes FTC compliance assistance and audit logs, and is built on Cloudflare to ensure your brand benefits from sub-second load times and high uptime.
- **Success-Aligned Model**: Our platform is designed to replace your costly agency retainers and static forms with a system where your pricing is directly aligned with your success.

Most brands like yours can go live with a production-grade, compliant intake journey in under a week.
