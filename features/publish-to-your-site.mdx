You can publish an Embeddable in three ways:

1. **Frontend embed** — add the embed code to your site (HTML, React, React Native, or a site builder).
2. **Reverse proxy** — serve the Embeddable through your own domain.
3. **Backend / SSR** — integrate with the Embeddables API from your server.

## Reverse proxy

Serve Embeddables content through your domain in a few steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Point your page at the proxy">
    Point your reverse proxy to `https://proxy.embeddables.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the proxy page">
    Open `https://yourdomain.com/your-reverse-proxy-page` and confirm it looks like `https://proxy.embeddables.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pass the Embeddable ID">
    Send the Embeddable ID with the `X-Embeddables-Id` header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional metadata headers">
    - `X-Embeddables-Title` — page title.
    - `X-Embeddables-Description` — meta description.
    - `X-Embeddables-Favicon` — favicon URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it works">
    You should see your Embeddable on the proxied page.
  </Step>
</Steps>
